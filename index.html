About

I am a PhD candidate in Marketing at Drexel University's LeBow College of Business. My current research focuses on social media environments, the content individuals encounter, and the emotional and psychological experiences that arise from these interactions.

My research is motivated by questions with implications for policy and practice. I am particularly interested in how insights developed in one area of research can help address questions in another. I also enjoy the flexibility of working with different methods and thinking through how to approach a problem.
